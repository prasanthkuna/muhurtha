Privacy Policy

Effective: 6 July 2026 · App: Muhūrta (app.muhurta)

This policy describes how Muhūrta (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Android mobile application and related services.

Who we are

Developer: Prashanth Kuna. Contact: prasanthkuna@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account & phone: Mobile number for one-time-password (OTP) sign-in via Supabase Auth.
  • Birth & profile: Optional name, date/time of birth, birth place, current city, language preference, and chart inputs (e.g. Janma Nakshatra when provided).
  • Location: With your permission, coarse or fine device location to resolve city/timezone for timing calculations.
  • App content: Your birth intelligence pack, Today cards, Life Map chapters, Timing preferences, and Ask questions/answers stored in our database.
  • Subscriptions: Purchase and entitlement status via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing (we do not receive your full payment card number).
  • Device & logs: Basic technical logs (app version, errors) to keep the service reliable.
  • Notifications: If enabled, we schedule local reminders on your device; we do not sell notification data.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and maintain your profile.
  • Compute and personalize Jyotish timing, Decode, Life Map, and Ask responses.
  • Process subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • Improve reliability, security, and support.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers that help run the app:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and backend functions.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Google Play — billing and distribution.
  • Cloud AI providers — to generate chart-based content when you use Decode, Life Map, Today, Timing, or Ask (prompts are tied to your profile, not posted publicly).

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. You may request deletion in the app (Profile → Account) or at muhurta-legal.vercel.app/delete-account. Email prasanthkuna@gmail.com. We will delete or anonymize personal data unless we must retain it for legal obligations.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location permission in Android settings (some timing features may be limited).
  • Disable notifications in app or system settings.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion via email.

Children

Muhūrta is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted transport, access controls). No method is 100% secure.

International transfers

Data may be processed in India and other countries where our providers operate, with appropriate safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Effective” date above will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
