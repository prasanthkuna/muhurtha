Refund & cancellation policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy covers Muhūrta Pro bought on the Muhūrta web app (billed by Razorpay).

Cancelling

  • Cancel any time in the app: Settings › Your plan › Cancel auto-renewal, or email us and we will cancel it for you.
  • Cancelling stops all future renewals. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for, then your account moves to Free.
  • Revoking the UPI Autopay mandate in your UPI app also stops future renewals.
  • Your charts, readings and history are never deleted when Pro ends.

Refunds

  • Payments for a period that has started are generally not refundable, and there are no partial refunds for unused days.
  • Full refund if you were charged twice for the same period, charged after you cancelled, or could not use Pro because of a problem on our side — write to us within 7 days of the charge.
  • Subscribed by mistake and have not used any Pro feature? Write within 48 hours of the first payment for a full refund.
  • Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.
  • Pro bought through Google Play follows Google Play's refund policy.

Failed renewals

If a renewal fails, Razorpay retries for a few days and Pro stays on for up to 3 days. If it still fails, the account moves to Free; nothing else is charged.

Contact

Email prasanthkuna@gmail.com with the phone number or email on your account and the payment date. We reply within 2 working days.
