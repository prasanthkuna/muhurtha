Pricing

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Muhūrta is a Jyotish (Vedic astrology) timing companion. Most of it is free. Muhūrta Pro adds family charts, every language and unlimited timing.

Free — ₹0

  • Your own birth chart, Today, Decode and your current Life chapter
  • Daily 6:45 AM brief and timing alerts
  • Share cards
  • 3 Ask questions a day; 3 Timing checks and 3 Find-a-date searches a day
  • Your reading in the language you choose at sign-up

Pro — ₹99 / month or ₹499 / year

  • Everything in Free
  • Family and friend charts (up to 10)
  • Change language any time (English, Telugu, Hindi)
  • Unlimited Timing checks and Find-a-date
  • Up to 30 Ask questions a day (fair use)
  • Every Life chapter, weekly and monthly guidance

Billing

  • Prices are in Indian Rupees (INR) and include all applicable taxes.
  • Pro on the web app (pwa-lyart-phi.vercel.app) is billed by Razorpay via UPI Autopay, card or netbanking. Pro in the Android app is billed by Google Play.
  • Pro renews automatically every month or year until you cancel. There is no free trial.
  • Cancel any time in Settings › Your plan; you keep Pro until the end of the paid period. See the Refund & cancellation policy.

Delivery

Muhūrta is a digital service; nothing is shipped. Pro unlocks in your account immediately after payment.
