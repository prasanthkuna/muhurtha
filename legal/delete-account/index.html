Delete your account & data

App: Muhūrta (app.muhurta)

You can request deletion of your Muhūrta account and associated personal data at any time.

What we delete

  • Profile and display name
  • Birth date, time window, place, and chart inputs
  • Birth intelligence packs, Today cards, Life Map, and Ask history
  • Notification preferences

What may be retained briefly

  • Billing records required by law or Google Play (we do not store card numbers)
  • Anonymized technical logs for security (no birth chart content)

How to request

In the app: Profile & timing → Account → Email deletion request

By email: Send a message from the phone number on your account to prasanthkuna@gmail.com with subject Muhūrta account deletion request.

We verify ownership via your registered phone number and complete deletion within 7 days, then confirm by email when possible.

Active subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel Google Play subscriptions. Cancel in Play Store → Payments & subscriptions before or after requesting deletion.
