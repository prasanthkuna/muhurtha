# Pricing research verdict for **Muhūrta**

## Market reality

Indian astrology market is mostly priced in **3 ways**:

| Model                                 | Examples                              |                Market price |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------: |
| **Free + ads + upsell**               | AstroSage                             |  Mostly free, huge adoption |
| **Per-minute astrologer calls/chats** | AstroTalk, Astroyogi                  |        ~₹12/min to ₹64/min+ |
| **Premium reports / AI questions**    | Co–Star, report apps                  | ₹299–₹1,199 per pack/report |
| **API backend providers**             | VedicRishi, VedicAstroAPI, APIReports |       ₹899–₹8,999/month B2B |

AstroTalk has **5Cr+ downloads** and pushes free first chat/call, then wallet recharge and per-minute astrologer consultation. ([Google Play][1])
Astroyogi lists astrologers around **₹12/min to ₹64/min** depending on astrologer and deal pricing. ([Astroyogi][2])
AstroSage Kundli has **5Cr+ downloads**, many free features, ads, in-app purchases, AI Kundli, Panchang, horoscope, matching, and talk-to-astrologer. ([AstroSage][3])
Co–Star India shows premium in-app purchases like **5 Questions ₹299**, **Pro-Star monthly ₹999**, **Advanced Chart ₹999**, and **Year Ahead ₹1,199**. ([App Store][4])
Vedic astrology API providers price B2B access from about **₹899/month** to **₹8,999/month**, while VedicAstroAPI starts Vedic APIs around **₹1,499/month** and PDF reports from **₹2/PDF**. ([Astrology Api Reports][5])

---

# What this means for us

Muhūrta should **not** price like AstroTalk.

AstroTalk/Astroyogi sell:

```text
Human astrologer time
emotional reassurance
chat/call dependency
```

Muhūrta sells:

```text
daily decision timing
past-proof trust
purpose-based good/caution windows
phase-wise Jyotish sentences
private self-serve guidance
```

So we should avoid expensive per-minute pricing and avoid looking like a generic horoscope subscription.

---

# Best pricing strategy

## Use freemium + low-friction subscription + one-time packs

```text
Free = trust + habit
Plus = daily power use
Pro = serious planning
Packs = high-intent moments
```

This fits Indian audience better than one expensive subscription.
